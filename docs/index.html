When the models go down, you get told to touch grass.

Status pages last checked 10 Sep 2026 08:00 UTC. Every feed carries this time even when nothing has happened, so a check time that stops moving means the collector has died rather than that the models are behaving.

Paste one of those URLs into any feed reader. RSS has no per-subscriber settings, so the maintenance toggle is a choice of URL. Only providers on the statuspage and rss adapters publish maintenance at all; for the rest, feed.xml and outages.xml carry the same items.
